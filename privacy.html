Privacy

Commission Board · last updated 14 November 2026

What we store

When you create an account we store your email address, a password (Supabase stores it as a secure hash, so nobody, including us, can read it) and the display name and default currency you choose. When you use the board we store the commissions you add: client name, description, deadline, price, currency, payment status and which column the card is in.

Who can see it

Your commissions are private to your account. The database only lets a signed-in user read or change their own rows, so other designers using Commission Board cannot see your board. We don’t sell your data or share it with anyone, and there are no ads or trackers on this site.

Where it’s kept

Accounts and data are hosted by Supabase. The site itself is hosted on GitHub Pages.

Emails

We only email you to confirm your address or reset your password.

Deleting your data

You can delete any card at any time. To delete everything, open the account menu (your initials, top right) and choose Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all of your commissions.
